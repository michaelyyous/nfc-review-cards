## Verification checklist when samples arrive

- [ ] **NXP TagInfo** (free, iOS/Android): ECDSA originality signature present? GET_VERSION responds? Chip reports as NTAG213?
- [ ] **UIDs unique** across all samples — duplicates mean clones
- [ ] **iPhone background read** — tap without opening an app; banner should appear. Test on an iPhone XS or newer
- [ ] **Android read** with screen on and unlocked
- [ ] **Read range** — should be several cm, not "touching exactly the right spot"
- [ ] **No metal** in the base (magnet test)
- [ ] **Print quality** — colour accuracy, hard-coat/lamination, no smudging under a thumbnail
- [ ] **Rewritable** — can you change the URL? (should be yes, with the password)
- [ ] **Not rewritable without the password** — try from a second phone with NFC Tools; it should refuse
